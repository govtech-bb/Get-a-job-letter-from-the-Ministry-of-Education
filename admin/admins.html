Admins

People who can sign in to this console. Adding someone here lets them request a sign-in code for their work email.

About these roles
Admin
  • View the dashboard and all issued letters.
  • Add, edit, deactivate and reactivate employees.
  • View the change history for any employee.
Super admin
Everything an admin can do, plus:
  • Add new admins and remove existing ones.
  • Promote an admin to super admin, or change a super admin back.
  • Deactivate or reactivate another admin's sign-in access.

At least one active super admin must remain at all times. Super admins cannot change their own role or deactivate their own account — ask another super admin to do it for you.
