Cookies

Cookies are small files saved on your phone, tablet or computer when you visit a website. We use cookies to make this service work.

What we use cookies for

We only set strictly necessary cookies. We do not use any cookies for analytics or advertising.

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies keep the service working. They are not used to track you.

  • moe_admin_session — only set if you sign in to the admin console. Remembers that you've signed in for up to 8 hours. Cleared when you sign out.

Because we don't use any non-essential cookies, you don't need to choose whether to accept them.
