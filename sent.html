What happens next

  1. Open the email from noreply@moe.gov.bb. It should arrive within a few minutes.
    Not in your inbox after a few minutes? Check your spam or junk folder.
  2. Download the attached PDF letter.
  3. Forward or print the letter to share with your bank, credit union or retailer.
If the email doesn't arrive
  • Check your spam or junk folder.
  • Wait 5 minutes — most emails arrive within seconds but it can occasionally take longer.
  • If it's still not there, request another letter.
  • If you keep having trouble, contact your school's personnel officer or the Ministry's Personnel Department.
