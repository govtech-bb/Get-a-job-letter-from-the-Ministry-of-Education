Who can use this service

This service is for current employees of the Ministry of Education Transformation. It generates a job letter from the records the Ministry holds about you.

What you must not do

  • Request a letter for somebody other than yourself.
  • Alter the contents of a letter after it has been issued. The QR code on each letter is checked when scanned; tampered letters fail verification.
  • Use the service to attempt to access systems or data you are not entitled to.

Reliance on letters

Banks, retailers and other parties relying on a letter should scan the QR code or visit the verification link printed on the letter to confirm it is genuine and current.
